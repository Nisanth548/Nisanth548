### 📌 My Projects
🌾 Featured Project

RootGuard – AI-Based Early Waterlogging Prediction and Prevention System

An AI-powered agricultural solution designed to predict and prevent waterlogging before serious crop damage occurs.

🌍 Problem Statement

Heavy rainfall and poor field drainage can cause waterlogging in agricultural fields. Excess water reduces oxygen availability around plant roots, damages root systems, increases disease risks, and reduces crop productivity.

Farmers often identify waterlogging only after visible crop damage has started.

💡 Our Solution

RootGuard aims to predict the risk of agricultural waterlogging using soil moisture, rainfall information, and AI-based risk analysis.

The system aims to help farmers take preventive action before serious crop damage occurs.

⚡ Key Features

🌱 Soil Moisture Monitoring

🌧️ Rainfall Prediction and Analysis

🤖 AI-Based Waterlogging Risk Prediction

📊 Field Waterlogging Risk Analysis

🔔 Early Warning Notifications

💧 Recommended Drainage Actions

📱 Farmer-Friendly Mobile Application

📱 Example Alert
